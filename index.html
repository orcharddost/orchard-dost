About Orchard Dost

Technology with a purpose: supporting farmers.

Orchard Dost is an agriculture technology initiative focused on farmers of Jammu & Kashmir. Our aim is to make useful agricultural knowledge, orchard planning tools and digital support easier to access.

Starting with apple cultivation, our vision is to support more fruit crops and farming communities across the region.
